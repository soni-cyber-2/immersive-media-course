## Course Plan: Introduction to Immersive Media (CDES408)

This course introduces B.Des students to the fundamentals of immersive media, including spatial design, perception, interaction, presence and immersive storytelling. Students will use **A-Frame** as the primary prototyping environment to implement and experience these concepts.

**Learning progression:** Foundations → Augmented Reality → Virtual Reality → 360° Video

---

### 🧩 Module 1: Foundations of XR and 3D Space

**Week 1: Introduction to Immersive Media**
- Understanding AR, VR, MR, XR and 360° Media
- Immersion, Presence and Physical–Digital Space
- Experiencing and Comparing Immersive Media

**Week 2: Understanding 3D Space**
- Exploring 3D Space with A-Frame
- 3D Coordinate Systems, Position, Rotation and Scale
- Camera, Viewpoint and Perspective vs Orthographic Projection
- Depth, Distance and Object Relationships

**Week 3: Building a 3D Scene**
- 3D Primitives, Models and Scene Elements
- Parent–Child Relationships and Object Hierarchy
- Ground, Sky and Environmental Setup
- Materials, Textures, Lighting and Shadows

**Week 4: Movement and Response**
- Animation and Object Movement
- Events and Basic Interaction
- Visual Feedback through Colour and Visibility
- Sound and Audio Response

---

### 🧩 Module 2: Designing Physical–Digital Space with Augmented Reality

**Week 5: Mobile AR — Building the Experience**
- Placing Digital Objects in Physical Space
- Scale, Distance and Orientation
- Viewpoint and Environmental Context
- Building a Basic Mobile AR Prototype

**Week 6: Mobile AR — Interaction and Feedback**
- Tap, Select and Trigger-Based Interaction
- Visual Feedback and Information Reveal
- Animation, Response and Interaction Cues

**Week 7: Passthrough AR / Mixed Reality — Building the Experience**
- Spatial Placement in the User’s Environment
- Body Scale and Personal Space
- Walking Around Digital Objects
- Near and Far Spatial Relationships

**Week 8: Passthrough AR / Mixed Reality — Interaction and Feedback**
- Gaze, Pointing and Selection
- Controller-Based Interaction
- Near and Far Interaction
- Reaching, Feedback and Spatial Response

---

### 🧩 Module 3: Designing Immersive Space with Virtual Reality

**Week 9: Building a VR Environment**
- Human Scale and Proportion in VR
- Object Placement, Lighting and Shadows
- Creating a Simple Immersive Environment

**Week 10: Selection and Interaction in VR**
- Controller-Based Pointing, Selection and Object Manipulation
- Gaze-Based Selection and Other Input Methods
- Spatial UI: Panels, Buttons, Labels and Menus

**Week 11: Navigation and Locomotion in VR**
- Teleportation and Continuous Locomotion
- Orientation and Movement through Virtual Space
- Wayfinding, Landmarks and Navigation Cues

**Week 12: Designing the VR Experience**
- Entry, Exploration and Progression through Space
- User Journey and Guiding Attention
- Integrating Space, Interaction and Navigation

---

### 🧩 Module 4: Viewpoint and Storytelling with 360° Video

**Week 13: Understanding 360° Video**
- 360° Video, Equirectangular Media and Spherical Viewing
- Viewer Orientation and Head Movement
- Presence in a Captured Environment

**Week 14: Composition and Attention**
- Composition Without a Fixed Frame
- Visual Anchors and Directional Cues
- Guiding Attention with Sound and Spatial Audio

**Week 15: Immersive Storytelling**
- Narrative Sequence and Scene Progression
- Combining Visual and Audio Cues
- Creating a Short 360° Immersive Experience
